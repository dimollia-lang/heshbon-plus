Swaplanet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Swaplanet ("the game") is a casual arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information is processed when you play it.

Information the game itself stores

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your best score, collected stars, unlocked skins and the sound setting are saved only on your device (local app storage). We do not receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some rounds and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue a round. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information about the app and ads (for example, crash and performance data).

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Skins → Privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The game requests internet and network-state access (to load ads). The Google Mobile Ads SDK also declares the advertising ID permission and Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution, topics), which are controlled by your device's ad privacy settings. The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
